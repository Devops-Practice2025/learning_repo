-  Design, implement, and maintain robust and scalable CI/CD pipelines to automate software builds,
testing, and deployments.

optimal performance.
Containerization & Orchestration:
- Work with container technologies like Docker for packaging applications.

System Monitoring & Troubleshooting:
- Build and sustain critical monitoring, logging, and ing infrastructure with Grafana, and Splunk to
deliver real- time visibility into application and system performance.
- Proactively identify and resolve technical issues, perform root cause analysis of production errors, and
implement preventive measures.
- Design procedures for system troubleshooting and maintenance.
Collaboration & Communication:
- Collaborate within a project manager & technical lead to ensure alignment of DevOps practices with
program objectives
- Collaborate closely with our European counterparts.
- Act as a liaison between development, operations, and other cross- functional teams to foster a culture
of collaboration and shared responsibility.
- Communicate technical issues and solutions effectively to both technical and non- technical
stakeholders.
- Participate in code reviews and contribute to improving development practices.
Required Skills and Qualifications:
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Information Technology, or a related field (or
equivalent practical experience).
- 4 to 6 years of experience as a DevOps Engineer.
- Possesses excellent English communication skills, crucial for seamless interaction with internal teams
and external stakeholders.
- Strong understanding of DevOps principles, methodologies, and best practices.
- Proficiency with version control systems, especially Git and Git workflows (e.g., GitHub, GitLab).
- Hands- on experience with at least one major cloud platform preferably Openshift.
- Expertise with CI/CD tool -  Jenkins
- Proficiency in scripting languages (e.g., Python or Shell).
- Expertise in containerization (Docker) and Kubernetes orchestration
- Expertise and with practical experience in crafting Helm charts.
- Hands- on experience with Artifactory for artifact management and binary repositories.
- Hands- on experience in implementing and managing SonarQube for code quality and security analysis.
- Familiarity with monitoring and logging tools -  Grafana & Splunk
- Understanding of networking concepts & system administration (Linux/Windows).
- Excellent problem- solving, analytical, and troubleshooting skills.
- Strong communication and interpersonal skills, with a collaborative team spirit.
